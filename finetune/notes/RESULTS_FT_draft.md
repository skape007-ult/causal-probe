# Fine-tuning Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct on Corr2Cause: what it learned (behaviour, E1–E5)

**Setup.** Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, LoRA rank 16, 3 epochs on 7,184 Corr2Cause items per condition. Conditions and
seeds:
- **A:** official data, 5 seeds.
- **B:** balanced by hypothesis string, premise and length, 5 seeds.
- **A48:** official data at B's exposure to the tested strings, 3 seeds.
- **C:** each item's hypothesis and label on another item's premise, 3 seeds.
- **Bswap:** B with premises swapped, 3 registered seeds.

**Statistic.** S is the within-hypothesis-string AUROC with prompt length removed (PREREG Section 3). Every test is
the single registered test, pooling seeds over seed and premise variance. "Claimed" means Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 over
the eleven registered tests. Pre-registration: `finetune/PREREG.md`, version 2 plus Amendment 1, reviewed
independently twice before any training.

**Gate and control.**
- **E1 passed:** A does the task in distribution. S on ID48 is 0.846 (threshold 0.80); pooled AUROC on ID is 0.980
  (threshold 0.922).
- **E2c passed (negative control):** models trained on premise-swapped B have S − 0.5 = −0.004 [−0.034, +0.026] on
  POOL-LB (seeds 0–2).

## Claim 1. On the debiased pool, training on balanced data gives higher S than official data. That this is more than exposure to the tested strings is not shown.
- **E2a, claimed:** S(B) − S(A) on POOL-LB = +0.138 [+0.114, +0.161], Holm p < 0.001.
- **E2b, not shown:** S(B) − S(A48) = +0.077 [+0.024, +0.130], Holm p 0.068 [audit 11].
- Per-seed S on POOL-LB: A 0.760–0.794, A48 0.816–0.856, B 0.905–0.924.
- **Prediction failed:** S(A) on POOL-LB was predicted at 0.50–0.65; it is 0.778 (mean of 5 seeds) [audit 11].

## Claim 2. Neither model is shown to exceed copying from similar training premises together with a text-only program fitted on its own training data.
The decisive comparison (standing order 6) is each model against the memorisation reference of its own training set.
- **B, E5b, not shown.** S − memorisation reference (0.898) = +0.018 [−0.006, +0.043], Holm p 0.21.
- **A, E5b, claimed, with a qualification** [audit 9]. A is claimed above the registered memorisation reference:
  +0.075 [+0.041, +0.109], Holm p 0.018. It is not above the pair-only variant of Amendment 1: +0.009
  [−0.023, +0.041], p 0.49.
  - That variant's best member is the distance-weighted rule with τ = 4. The amendment's quoted 0.745 is its τ = 2
    member.
  - A copy rule just outside the registered grid (pair-only, τ = 8; not registered) scores 0.778, equal to A's mean S.
- **E5c, claimed below for both.** Both models are below the best text reference fitted on their own training set,
  which is the tuned stack in both cases:

  | Model | Tuned stack S | S − tuned stack |
  |---|---|---|
  | A | 0.846 | −0.068 [−0.095, −0.041] |
  | B | 0.956 | −0.040 [−0.058, −0.022] |

  L3 is not reached by either model.
- **E5a, claimed for both.** Both track validity inside cells of string × adjacency: S − 0.5 is +0.179 for A and
  +0.366 for B.
- **Ladder (PREREG Section 0):**
  - **A: L2.** It is above the registered copy reference only, not above the pair-only variant, so A is not described
    as going beyond copying.
  - **B: L1.**
- **A48, descriptive, intervals only, no test** [audit 7, 11]:
  - S − memorisation reference: +0.082 [+0.016, +0.149].
  - S − best text reference: −0.046 [−0.105, +0.014].
  - S in cells − 0.5: +0.271 [+0.174, +0.367]. Its seed SD of 0.036 exceeds 0.03, so this interval is underpowered.

## Claim 3. On held-out premises, renaming the variables lowers S as the authors reported; the drop is not shown to be due to the reversal of letter order within a hypothesis string.
For A on ID48, within seed:
- **E4a, claimed:** S(original) − S(renamed A…F → Z…U) = +0.074 [+0.046, +0.101], Holm p 0.012.
- **E4b, not shown:** S(renamed keeping order, A…F → U…Z) − S(renamed reversing) = +0.001 [−0.016, +0.017], Holm p
  0.92. Any effect of letter order is bounded at about ±0.017 [audit 11].
- **Prediction failed:** E4b was predicted to be at least half of E4a [audit 11].

## Deviations from the pre-registration
- **E1:** "F1 at the threshold that maximises F1 on the training set" was not computed. Training-set scores were not
  stored, and it does not affect the gate [audit 5].
- **E5 near/far:** split as described above [audit 8].
- **Reference grids at their edge:**
  - The registered copy family's best τ is the top of its grid (4) in every condition.
  - The tuned stack's best C is the top of its grid (100). C = 1000 would raise it by 0.001, which only strengthens
    the "below" claims.
