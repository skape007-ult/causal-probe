## Read this first [audit 2]
The probe is trained on 2,346 **gold labels from the selection half of POOL-LB itself** (in distribution). The
models' answers come from training elsewhere (A, B), or from no task training at all (base). "Probe above answer"
therefore does **not** mean "information present and not used". It means a linear readout trained on this pool's
labels beats the model's own answer. A text-only program trained on the same labels beats the probe in every
condition. Nothing is drawn from the base row.

## Layers [audit 3]
- The registered criterion "first layer whose interval lies above 0.5" is layer 1 in every condition and position.
  Layer 0 (embeddings at one token) is at 0.50 by construction. This criterion does not separate anything, and the
  text does **not** say the label "becomes decodable" at layer 1.
- "First layer reaching 90% of the maximum S" (registered): 8–16, depending on condition and position.
- "First layer reaching 90% of the maximum excess over 0.5" is **not registered**. It is an extra column, and its
  selection-half version is the site rule of Amendment 3.
- The curves rise steeply between layers 7 and 16 at both positions, for every condition.

## Adjacency [audit 7]
S_cells (string × adjacency) is reported, but it is not cited as evidence about adjacency: adjacency alone scores
0.50 on this pool, by its construction.

## Choices the code made where Amendment 2 left details open [audit 5]
- Within-string correlation: Spearman on raw scores (not length-residualised), weighted by items per string, for
  strings with at least 5 items.
- The layer choice and the C choice share the selection half's folds. C is chosen by v1.0's inner CV on the whole
  selection half (scored by accuracy, v1.0's criterion), and the layer by 3-fold out-of-fold S at that C.
- 500 premise resamples for the per-layer curves; 2,000 (as in Section 3) for every summary row.
- The logistic solver does not fully converge in 300 iterations at C = 1 (v1.0's setting). The auditor's
  recomputations match to about ±0.01 in those cells and exactly elsewhere.
